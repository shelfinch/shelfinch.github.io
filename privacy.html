Shelfinch Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

The short version: Shelfinch collects nothing about you. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no servers of ours for your data to reach.

Your inventory stays on your devices

Everything you put into Shelfinch — items, quantities, prices, dates — is stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, Apple's private CloudKit moves that data between your own devices under your Apple account; we cannot read it and have no access to it.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode, Shelfinch sends only the barcode number to the public, non-profit Open Food Facts database to suggest a product name. Nothing about you, your device, or your list is included, and nothing is stored there by us.

Notifications

Restock reminders are local notifications, generated and scheduled on your device. They involve no network activity.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Links to Amazon

"Buy on Amazon" buttons open Amazon in your browser or Amazon app. These links may include an affiliate tag, which tells Amazon the visit came from Shelfinch and may earn us a small commission at no cost to you. What happens on Amazon is governed by Amazon's own privacy policy.

What we would have to change

If a future feature ever needs to handle any personal data, this policy will say so plainly before that feature ships, and the app will ask first.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@shelfinch.app
